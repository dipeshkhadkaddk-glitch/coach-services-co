# 🚀 Future Roadmap: Path to Grand Success
**Application:** Coach Services Co. — Brisbane Olympics 2032 Edition

To transition this platform from a logistics tool to a **world-class Olympic experience**, we recommend the following strategic upgrades.

## 1. Real-Time "Live Track" GPS
**The Concept:** Give users the "Uber experience" for their coach.
- **How it works:** Drivers use a mobile app that streams their GPS coordinates to the server. 
- **The Value:** Users can see the live location of their shuttle on a map in their dashboard, reducing "where is my bus?" anxiety during busy event days.

## 2. Integrated Apple & Google Wallet Tickets
**The Concept:** Seamless entry without opening the website.
- **How it works:** When a booking is confirmed, users receive a link to add their ticket to their digital wallet.
- **The Value:** Professional, contactless boarding. Drivers simply scan the iPhone/Android watch as the passenger enters.

## 3. AI-Powered Dynamic Pricing
**The Concept:** Maximizing revenue during high-demand events.
- **How it works:** An AI algorithm monitors booking frequency for specific events (like the Opening Ceremony or 100m Final).
- **The Value:** Prices automatically increase as the shuttle fills up, similar to airline or hotel pricing, ensuring the highest possible "Olympic Profit."

## 4. Multilingual Experience (Olympic Ready)
**The Concept:** Welcoming the world in their own language.
- **How it works:** Integration of an auto-translation layer (Google Cloud Translate API).
- **The Value:** Tourists from Japan, France, Brazil, and China can use the app in their native language, making your service the most accessible in Brisbane.

## 5. "Gold Medal" Loyalty & VIP Program
**The Concept:** Rewarding frequent Olympic travelers.
- **How it works:** Users earn "Olympic Points" for every ride. 
- **The Value:** Points can be redeemed for "Front Seat" priority, free upgrades to "Luxury Coaches," or partner discounts at Olympic merchandise stores.

## 6. Smart Route Optimization
**The Concept:** Beating Brisbane's Olympic traffic.
- **How it works:** Integration with Google Maps Traffic API.
- **The Value:** If a major road is blocked by an Olympic parade, the system automatically sends a "New Route" notification to the driver's tablet and updates the passenger's arrival time.

## 7. Native Mobile Apps (iOS & Android)
**The Concept:** Moving beyond the browser.
- **How it works:** Developing native apps using Flutter or React Native.
- **The Value:** Ability to send **Push Notifications** (instead of just SMS), offline access to tickets, and better use of biometric security (FaceID).
